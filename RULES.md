# ⚡ Lakeshore Showdown — October 2026 Sales Spiff

**Chrysler Muskegon vs. Chrysler Grand Haven vs. Grand Haven Ford** · Oct 1–31, 2026 · **$10,500 prize pool** ($3,500 from each store) · Live leaderboard on your phone, updated every morning.

## The huddle version

Sell new cars. Hold used gross. Every deal moves **your store** up the board *and* puts **you** in the running for cash. Six ways to get paid, weekly bounties so every week matters, and a store battle scored on **% of your own store's target**, so a smaller store can beat a bigger one.

## Six ways to get paid (payout table)

| Prize | How to win it | Pays | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🏆 **Store Battle** ("The Throwdown") | Highest **store score**: 50% × (new units ÷ store's new target) + 50% × (used gross ÷ store's used-gross target) | 1st store **$3,000**, 2nd store **$1,500**, 3rd $0. Split evenly among the store's qualified reps | **$4,500** |
| 🏁 **New Unit King** | Most new units, all 3 stores on one board | **$1,000** / $600 / $400 | **$2,000** |
| 💰 **Used Gross Boss** | Most used-car front gross, all 3 stores | **$1,000** / $600 / $400 | **$2,000** |
| ⭐ **Store MVP** | Most Showdown Points *at your own store* (2 pts per new unit + 1 pt per $1,000 used gross) | **$250** × 3 stores | **$750** |
| 🎯 **Weekly Top Gun** | Most new units in the contest week | **$125** × 5 weeks | **$625** |
| 🐟 **Weekly Big Fish** | Biggest single used-car gross deal of the week | **$125** × 5 weeks | **$625** |
| **Total** | 4,500 + 2,000 + 2,000 + 750 + 625 + 625 | = 3 stores × $3,500 | **$10,500** |

- **Contest weeks:** W1 Oct 1–7 · W2 Oct 8–14 · W3 Oct 15–21 · W4 Oct 22–28 · W5 "Closing Bell" Oct 29–31.
- **Team pot:** split evenly among every rep on the winning store's roster with **at least 1 counted unit** (new + used, splits = 0.5). With 7 qualified reps: 1st = **$428.57 each**, 2nd = **$214.29 each**.
- **Stacking is allowed:** one rep can win team, individual, MVP and bounty money.
- **Why it's fair to smaller stores:** the store battle uses each store's own target, every store crowns its own MVP, and the board also shows per-rep averages.

## Deal-counting rules

- A deal counts on its **delivery date**: sold, delivered and posted in the DMS. **Retail only**; fleet, wholesale and dealer trades don't count. [A]
- **New units:** 1 per new vehicle. **Split deal = 0.5 each** (max 2 reps per deal).
- **Used gross** = front-end gross as posted in the DMS, after pack, **excluding F&I/back-end**. [A] Deals that lose money count as negative gross. A split used deal = 0.5 unit, and each rep gets **half the gross**.
- **Unwinds are removed** from the board, including from earlier weeks, and all standings and bounties recalculate. Unwinds after payout follow normal chargeback policy. [A]
- Store managers enter each day's deals **by 10 AM the next day** (the entry screen defaults to yesterday).
- **Final lock:** Wed **Nov 4, 5 PM** after each GM checks the board against the DMS. Paid on the next payroll as taxable wages. You must be employed on the payout date. [A]
- A rep who leaves keeps counting for the store total but can't win individual prizes.

## Tie-breaks (plain & final)

- **New Unit King:** more used gross wins. **Used Gross Boss:** more new units wins. **Store Battle:** higher new-unit % of target wins.
- **Top Gun:** more used gross that week. **Store MVP:** more new units. If still tied (or for **Big Fish**), the tied people **split** the prize money for the places they hold (two tied for 1st split 1st + 2nd).
- **Nothing goes unpaid:** a prize nobody qualifies for (e.g., no used deals in a week) rolls into the 1st-place store's team pot.

## Badges (bragging rights)

🎩 Hat Trick: 3+ new units in a day · 💣 Heavy Hitter: a $4,000+ used deal · 🔥 On Fire: a deal 3 selling days in a row · ⚡ Double Down: new + used on the same day · 🚀 Opening Day: a deal on Oct 1
