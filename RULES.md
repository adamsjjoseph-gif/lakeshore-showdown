# ⚡ Lakeshore Showdown · Sep 26–30, 2026

**Chrysler Muskegon vs. Chrysler Grand Haven vs. Grand Haven Ford** · Sat Sep 26 – Wed Sep 30, 2026 · ends **Wed Sep 30, 11:59:59 PM ET** · **$10,500 prize pool** ($3,500 from each store) · Live board + countdown on your phone.

## The huddle version

Five days. Sell new, hold used gross, **set appointments** (they count **Sat 9/26 – Tue 9/29**). Every deal and appointment moves **your store** up the board *and* puts **you** in the running for cash: six ways to get paid, a **Daily Hot Shot** every day, and a store battle scored on **% of your own store's targets**.

## Six ways to get paid (payout table)

| Prize | How to win it | Pays | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🏆 **Store Battle** ("The Throwdown") | Highest **store score** = ⅓ × (new units ÷ new target) + ⅓ × (used gross ÷ used-gross target) + ⅓ × (appointments ÷ appointment target) | 1st store **$2,500**, 2nd store **$1,000**, 3rd $0. Split evenly among the store's qualified reps | **$3,500** |
| 🏁 **New Unit King** | Most new units, all 3 stores on one board | **$2,000** to the top rep | **$2,000** |
| 💰 **Used Gross Boss** | Most used-car front gross, all 3 stores | **$2,000** to the top rep | **$2,000** |
| 📅 **Appointment Ace** | Most appointments set **Sat 9/26 – Tue 9/29**, all 3 stores | **$1,500** to the top rep | **$1,500** |
| ⭐ **Store MVP** | Most Showdown Points *at your own store* (2 pts per new unit + 1 pt per $1,000 used gross + 0.5 pt per appointment) | **$250** × 3 stores | **$750** |
| 🔥 **Daily Hot Shot** | Most Showdown Points **that day** (same points as Store MVP), all 3 stores | **$150** × 5 days (Sat 9/26 – Wed 9/30) | **$750** |
| **Total** | 3,500 + 2,000 + 2,000 + 1,500 + 750 + 750 | = 3 stores × $3,500 | **$10,500** |

- **Appointments** only count when dated **Sat 9/26 through Tue 9/29**. An appointment = a customer appointment the rep **set** that day and logged in the CRM, counted by the manager. [A] Appointments entered for Wed 9/30 don't count.
- **Team pot:** split evenly among every rep on the winning store's roster with **at least 1 counted unit** (new + used, splits = 0.5). With 7 qualified reps: 1st = **$357.14 each**, 2nd = **$142.86 each**.
- **Stacking is allowed:** one rep can win team, individual, MVP and Hot Shot money.

## Deal-counting rules

- A deal counts on its **delivery date**: sold, delivered and posted in the DMS. **Retail only**; fleet, wholesale and dealer trades don't count. [A]
- **New units:** 1 per new vehicle. **Split deal = 0.5 each** (max 2 reps per deal).
- **Used gross** = front-end gross as posted in the DMS, after pack, **excluding F&I/back-end**. [A] Deals that lose money count as negative gross. A split used deal = 0.5 unit, and each rep gets **half the gross**.
- **Unwinds are removed** from the board and all standings recalculate. Unwinds after payout follow normal chargeback policy. [A]
- Store managers enter each day's deals and appointments **the same night or by 10 AM the next day**.
- **Final lock:** Mon **Oct 5, 5 PM** after each GM checks the board against the DMS/CRM. [A] Paid on the next payroll as taxable wages. You must be employed on the payout date. [A]
- A rep who leaves keeps counting for the store total but can't win individual prizes.

## Tie-breaks (plain & final)

- **New Unit King:** more used gross wins. **Used Gross Boss:** more new units wins. **Appointment Ace:** more new units, then more used gross. **Store Battle:** higher new-unit % of target wins.
- **Daily Hot Shot:** more new units that day, then more used gross that day. **Store MVP:** more new units. If still tied, the tied people **split** the prize money.
- **Nothing goes unpaid:** a prize nobody qualifies for (e.g., the Hot Shot for **closed Sunday 9/27**) rolls into the 1st-place store's team pot.
