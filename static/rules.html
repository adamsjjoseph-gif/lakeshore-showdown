Deal-counting rules

  • A deal counts on its delivery date: sold, delivered and posted in the DMS. Retail only; fleet, wholesale and dealer trades don't count. [A]
  • New units: 1 per new vehicle. Split deal = 0.5 each (max 2 reps per deal).
  • Used gross = front-end gross as posted in the DMS, after pack, excluding F&I/back-end. [A] Deals that lose money count as negative gross. A split used deal = 0.5 unit, and each rep gets half the gross.
  • Unwinds are removed from the board and all standings recalculate. Unwinds after payout follow normal chargeback policy. [A]
  • The sales manager enters each day's deals and appointments the same night or by 10 AM the next day.
  • Final lock: Mon Oct 5, 5 PM after the GM checks the board against the DMS/CRM. [A] Paid on the next payroll as taxable wages. You must be employed on the payout date. [A]
  • A rep who leaves keeps counting for the store goal but can't win individual prizes.

Tie-breaks (plain & final)

  • Showdown Champion: more new units wins, then more used gross. New Unit King: more used gross wins. Used Gross Boss: more new units wins. Appointment Ace: more new units, then more used gross.
  • Daily Hot Shot: more new units that day, then more used gross that day. If still tied, the tied people split the prize money.
  • Nothing goes unpaid: a prize nobody qualifies for (e.g., the Hot Shot for closed Sunday 9/27) rolls into the Showdown Champion 1st-place prize. If only one rep earns points, they get 2nd-place money too.

Badges (bragging rights)

🎩 Hat Trick: 3+ new units in a day · 💣 Heavy Hitter: a $4,000+ used deal · 🔥 On Fire: a deal 3 selling days in a row · ⚡ Double Down: new + used on the same day · 🚀 Opening Day: a deal on Sep 26
